Activity 1: Budgeting for Cloud Services
Morena Realty moved to Amazon’s AWS cloud services last year. Their website and database is now cloud-based. The database stores documents, clients, and property histories. The website also accepts inquiries and enables bookings.

The company’s accountant would like to compare the cost of their previous on-premises infrastructure using historical data, to what they pay now for Amazon Web Services.

Use AWS pricing and services to describe and itemise the monthly costs of AWS.
Work out which services in the cloud could be accounting for most of these costs and explain the purpose they provide to the business.
Describe how the Alert system works for Amazon budgeting purposes and suggest to the accountant why and how you would set that up.
